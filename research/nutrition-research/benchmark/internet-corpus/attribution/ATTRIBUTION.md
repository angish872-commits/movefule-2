## Attribution requirements summary

- CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0 / CC BY 3.0 images require credit to the author, the licence name, and a link to the licence. The exact author and licence URL per file are in `attribution/attribution.json`.
- CC0 images (ic-013, ic-030) are dedicated to the public domain; attribution is optional but the creator is credited by convention.
- Where an app or derived product displays these images, the per-file credit must be shown; the CSV above (or `attribution.json`) is the authoritative mapping.
