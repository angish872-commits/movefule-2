# MoveFuel public-launch copy — draft for legal review

## General wellness notice

MoveFuel supports adults with general wellness, activity, and nutrition
tracking. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a
guarantee of nutrition accuracy. Consult a qualified health professional for
medical, dietary, allergy, pregnancy, eating-disorder, or medication-related
questions. Do not use MoveFuel for emergencies.

## Camera and nutrition notice

If enabled, photo-based meal estimates are estimates only. Review every food,
portion, and nutrient result before confirming it. Package labels, barcode
data, and your verified portion measurements take priority over a visual
estimate. MoveFuel does not confirm meals automatically.

## Image-processing consent

With your permission, MoveFuel processes meal photos to prepare a nutrition
estimate. Photos are stored privately, accessible only to you and authorised
MoveFuel services, and are deleted according to the published retention policy
or when you delete your account. Do not upload images containing sensitive
personal information unrelated to your meal.

## Age and availability

MoveFuel is for adults aged 18 and over. The initial public release is offered
in the United States, United Kingdom, and Australia. Do not create an account
if you are under 18.
